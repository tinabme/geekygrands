🌻 Grandma's Garden Wisdom

Growing beautiful things, one season at a time

🌱

Start Small, Dream Big

Every master gardener started with a single seed. Don't be overwhelmed by big garden dreams - begin with a few pots on the windowsill or a small patch in the yard. Success breeds confidence, and before you know it, you'll be expanding your green kingdom!

- Garden Basics
💧

Water Wisdom

The best time to water is early morning - plants can drink up before the heat of the day. Stick your finger into the soil; if it's dry an inch down, it's time to water. Remember: it's better to water deeply less often than to sprinkle lightly every day.

- Watering Tips
🌿

Seasonal Gardening

Each season brings its own magic and tasks. Spring is for planting and hope, summer for tending and harvesting, fall for preparing and preserving, winter for planning and dreaming. Embrace the rhythm - your garden will thank you for it!

- Year-Round Care
🦋

Welcome the Helpers

A healthy garden is full of life! Plant flowers that attract bees and butterflies, leave space for beneficial insects, and remember that not every bug is a pest. Sometimes the best gardening advice is to step back and let nature do what it does best.

- Natural Balance
