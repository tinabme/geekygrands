Brown Betty Apple Pie

My Grandma always brought a scrumptious pie to family gatherings - brown betty was one of my favorites

⭐ 3 Stars 🔥 Bake: 45-55 mins 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Serves: 8 ⏰ Plan Ahead (Crust Chilling)

A Family Gathering Favorite

This Brown Betty Apple Pie holds such special memories for me. My grandmother would arrive at every family gathering with this beautiful pie, and we'd all gather around as she cut the first slice, watching that crumbly topping and perfectly spiced apples.

What makes this pie special is the brown sugar crumb topping that gets all golden and crunchy while the apples underneath become tender and sweet. It's like having apple pie and apple crisp all in one delicious dessert! 🥧✨

What You'll Need

🥧 For the Crust

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup butter (very cold)
  • ½ cup water (very cold)

🍎 For the Filling

  • 8-10 apples (sliced, cored, peeled)
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

🧈 For the Topping

  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar

Let's Create This Masterpiece

1

Make the Crust

In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Cut in cold butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs. Drizzle in cold water and mix with a wooden spoon until mixture forms a chunky ball.

2

Chill the Dough

Divide dough in half and shape into balls. Wrap in plastic and refrigerate 4 hours or overnight. This is crucial for a flaky crust!

3

Prepare the Crust

Roll out one dough ball on floured counter and place in pie pan. Save the other half for another pie or freeze it!

4

Make the Filling

Heat oven to 400°F. In a large bowl, toss all filling ingredients together. Pour into pie crust, mounting apples toward the center.

5

Create the Topping

In a medium bowl, use your fingers to mix topping ingredients (except white sugar) until crumbs form. Sprinkle evenly over pie, then sprinkle with white sugar.

6

Bake to Golden Perfection

Bake for 45 to 55 minutes until pie crust and crumb topping are golden brown and filling begins to bubble. Transfer to cooling rack.

Grandma's Secret Tips

🛡️

Protect the Crust

To avoid overcooking the crust rim, lay a little foil around the rim about ¾ of the way through cooking.

❄️

Cold is Key

Really cold butter and water are essential for that perfect flaky crust. Trust the process!

🍎

Apple Selection

Mix sweet and tart apples for the best flavor - Granny Smith and Honeycrisp work beautifully together.

⏰

Patience Pays

Don't skip the chilling time for the dough - it makes all the difference in texture!
