Melt-in-Your-Mouth Buttermilk Biscuits

Fluffy, easy, buttery, mouthwatering biscuits you can enjoy with any meal

⭐ 2 Stars 🔥 Bake: 12-16 mins 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Makes: 8-10 ❄️ Keep It Cold!

The Art of Perfect Biscuits

There's something magical about homemade buttermilk biscuits - the way they split open with steam rising, begging for a pat of butter and honey. These biscuits are the real deal, using a technique that creates the most incredible layers.

The secret is in keeping everything cold and working quickly. I use a cheese grater to shred the butter, which creates perfect little pockets that turn into flaky layers when baked. It's like having a little piece of heaven on your breakfast table! 🧈✨

What You'll Need

🥄 Simple Perfection

  • 2 cups self-raising flour (sifted)
  • 1 stick real butter (very cold)
  • 1 cup buttermilk (very cold)

Let's Make Magic Happen

1

Prep and Chill

Preheat your oven to 450°F. Shred the stick of butter using a cheese grater and place it in the freezer while the oven heats up. This keeps it extra cold!

2

Mix Gently

Spoon flour into the bowl with the shredded butter and gently mix so the butter pieces are coated with flour. This prevents the butter from clumping together.

3

Add the Buttermilk

Pour in the cold buttermilk and stir just until a dough forms. Don't overmix - lumpy is perfectly fine!

4

Work Quickly

Sprinkle flour on the counter and dump the dough onto it. Flour your hands and gently pat and fold the dough several times to create layers. Flatten to about 1 inch thick. Work quickly to keep the dough from warming!

5

Cut and Bake

Cut out the biscuits and place them in a greased skillet or pie pan so their sides are touching. Bake for 12-16 minutes until cooked through and golden.

Grandma's Secret Tips

❄️

Cold Everything

Keep the dough cold and slightly sticky. This is the secret to incredibly flaky layers!

✂️

Straight Down Cut

Don't twist the cutter when cutting biscuits - press straight down to avoid sealing the sides.

🤗

Sides Touching

Place biscuits with sides touching in the pan - they support each other and rise higher!

🔥

Hot Oven Ready

Make sure the oven is fully preheated. You can chill the cut biscuits in the fridge while waiting!

Make It Your Own

🧀 Cheese Biscuits

Add ½ cup sharp cheddar cheese to the flour mixture for savory cheese biscuits.

🌿 Herb Biscuits

Mix in 2 tablespoons fresh chives, rosemary, or thyme for an aromatic twist.

🍯 Sweet Biscuits

Add 2 tablespoons sugar and a teaspoon of vanilla for a slightly sweet version.
